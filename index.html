Into the arena

Bazooka Noodles is a fast, cartoony worm arena game for Android by xBitsNL Gaming. Wiggle around a muddy arena, gobble up glowing food to grow longer, and make rival worms crash into your body. Then grab a crate, pull out a bazooka and blow your opponents down to size. Play against a full arena of computer worms on Easy, Normal or Hard, or host a game and invite your friends with a room code.

What’s inside

Eat & grow

Gobble glowing food to grow longer and climb the leaderboard. Boost for speed, at the cost of a little length.

Bazookas, uzis & banana bombs

Open mystery crates for a bazooka, bouncing grenades, a banana bomb, a wild uzi or a shotgun that kicks you back.

Snip them short

Explosions cut worms in two: the tail bursts into food for you. Small worms are blown away entirely.

150 skins

Tiger stripes, lava, galaxies and crowns. Earn coins by playing to unlock them.

Plays in 9 languages: English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Hindi.

More from the game

How to play

  1. Steer with the joystick on the left half of the screen.
  2. Eat the glowing food to grow. Hold Boost to sprint.
  3. Trap rivals: if their head hits your body, they burst into food.
  4. Grab a crate and tap Fire to launch whatever is inside.
  5. Survive, top the leaderboard and spend your coins on new skins.

Before each round, pick how tough the bots are. Easy bots pay 50% coins, Normal 100% and Hard 150%. Your very first round is always Easy.

With friends: tap Play with friends, host a game and share the room code. Everyone taps Ready, the host picks how tough the bots are, then starts. On the same Wi-Fi your game shows up under Games nearby.
